AI Mock Interview

An AI interviewer asks questions out loud, listens to spoken answers and asks a follow-up, like a real panel. Paste a job description and the technical questions stay on its technologies.

  • Technical, HR or mixed; easy, medium or hard
  • Every answer scored on content, relevance, communication and confidence
  • What a strong answer would include, for every question
